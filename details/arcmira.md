# Arcmira: YouTube Transcript Search

YouTube transcript search and monitoring for your AI. [Arcmira](https://arcmira.com) understands YouTube and podcasts like it watched them: the exact quote, often who said it, the full transcript and sponsors.

## Features

- Find timestamped quotes and speaker appearances across channels.
- Search mentions, sponsors and recommendations.
- Retrieve transcripts and follow links to source videos.

## Setup

Use the [MCP setup guide](https://arcmira.com/docs/mcp-server) to connect an Arcmira account. The [API documentation](https://arcmira.com/docs) also covers account usage and limits.

A paid read uses credits from your plan, then your on-demand budget.
