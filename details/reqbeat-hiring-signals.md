## Overview

Reqbeat's remote MCP server gives AI agents access to hiring signals: search live job postings by role and geography, qualify the companies behind them, and subscribe to companies so new reqs are pushed to a webhook.

## Features

- Search live job postings by role, keyword, and geography
- Look up the companies behind postings for qualification
- Subscribe to companies and receive new reqs via webhook
- Remote streamable-HTTP server at `https://mcp.reqbeat.com/mcp` (API-key auth, free tier)
- Listed on the official MCP Registry as `com.reqbeat/hiring-signals`
